CRO Page Prioritization

Where the Conversion
Leaks Are

The primary conversion mechanism on this site is the coverage check, which acts as a gate before any plan or pricing information is shown. This forces users to commit to a qualification step without understanding the product or cost, and the homepage compounds the problem by presenting seven competing CTAs, diluting focus and delaying the path to conversion. The most valuable fix is to make coverage optional and surface plans and pricing upfront, reducing friction and building trust earlier in the journey.
